Role Overview

We are looking for a hands-on product design leader to shape Atria’s platform experience across analytics, creative workflows, and AI-powered features, while contributing to the evolution of our agentic creative product.

How we design

We're not a traditional design team. A few things that define how we work:

AI-native workflow. We use AI tools (LLMs, code-based prototyping, generative design) as core parts of our design process — not as nice-to-haves. For example, we build interactive mockups with web code to stress-test product decisions before committing to final designs, and sometimes go straight into the codebase to refine interaction details.

Thinking > deliverables. We care more about the quality of product thinking than the volume of design output. A well-reasoned wireframe that clarifies the right problem beats a polished screen that solves the wrong one.

Ownership over the outcome. Designers here define what success looks like for their features — not just how they look. You'll set product goals, decide how to measure them, ship, track actual results, and iterate based on what you learn.

If you're the kind of designer who's excited about pushing the boundaries of how design work gets done — and you want to shape the product, not just style it — this is the role.

What you'll do

Drive product outcomes, not just design output.

Own features end-to-end: from identifying the user problem, to defining goals and success metrics, to designing, shipping, and iterating based on real performance data.

Make product decisions — not just UX recommendations. You'll work directly with the CEO and engineers to shape what we build and why.

Design for a complex, data-rich product.

Design analytics experiences, creative intelligence tools, and AI-driven workflows that help marketers move faster and make better decisions.

Navigate complex information architecture: multi-dimensional data, cross-platform ad accounts, competitive benchmarking, and AI-generated insights.

Evolve how we design.

Proactively explore and adopt AI tools and methods that improve your own — and the team's — design quality and speed.

Contribute to and evolve the design system to maintain consistency as the product scales.
